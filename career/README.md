# Career index

**Empty until you fill it.** This page is the map of your career record. Agents start here to find every collection and role; you can use it to see what is recorded so far. [How the record is organized](../schema/experience-schema.md).

Default: read-only. Records change only when you ask in words; see [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md#two-kinds-of-session).

## Pages

- [Career story](story.md): your whole career in plain language, newest first. Navigation for people, not evidence.
- [Goals and preferences](preferences.md): what you want next, your motivation, working preferences and standard portal answers. Separate from achievements.
- [Vocabulary](vocabulary.md): the function, capability and tool tags your records use.
- [Contact defaults](contact.json): used in résumé and cover-letter headers and portal fields.

## Collections

Grouped collections hold several roles in one file. An employer with many stories can have its own folder ([organization template](_templates/organization/)).

- [Work experience](experience/records.md): jobs and client engagements.
- [Education and credentials](education/records.md): degrees, diplomas, certificates and licenses.
- [Additional experience](additional/records.md): volunteering, teaching, side projects, creative work, sport and anything else that shows what you can do.

When you add a collection, list it here with one line on what it holds.
