# Work experience

[Career index](../README.md) · [Reference](../../schema/experience-schema.md) · [Blank record blocks](../_templates/grouped-collection/records.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** Jobs, contracts and client engagements, newest first: one role record per position, each followed by its experience records (one per thing you did) and any outcome records.

How it gets filled: your agent imports your CV first, one experience record per CV bullet, keeping the CV's wording as the starting account. Then, as you tell your story, it rewrites accounts from what you say and adds the limits (who owned what, what shipped). When one employer grows past a dozen stories, it can move to its own folder using the [organization template](../_templates/organization/).
