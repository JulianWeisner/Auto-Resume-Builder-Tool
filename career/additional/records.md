# Additional experience

[Career index](../README.md) · [Reference](../../schema/experience-schema.md) · [Blank record blocks](../_templates/grouped-collection/records.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** Everything else that shows what you can do: volunteering, teaching and coaching, side projects, freelance work, creative work, sport, community roles and early jobs. These often connect to a role in ways you would not expect, so record them even if they never appear on a résumé. Same format as work experience.
