# Education and credentials

[Career index](../README.md) · [Reference](../../schema/experience-schema.md) · [Blank record blocks](../_templates/grouped-collection/records.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** Degrees, diplomas, certificates and licenses, newest first. Use `record_type: "education"` for degrees and programs and `"credentials"` for certificates and licenses. Original dates stay as written; an expired certificate is recorded as expired, not as a current license. Coursework, a thesis or a teaching assistantship can have its own experience record when it is evidence for a job.
