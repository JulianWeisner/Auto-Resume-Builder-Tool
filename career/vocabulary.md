# Vocabulary: functions, capabilities and tools

[Career index](README.md) · [Reference](../schema/experience-schema.md#functions-capabilities-and-tools)

**Empty until you fill it.** This is the single list of tags your records may use. Tags let an agent find every story that matches what a job asks for, for example all experiences tagged `FN-operations`. A tag is navigation: it shows where to look, and the record's account is what proves the skill.

How it gets filled: while importing your CV and telling your story, your agent proposes tags from your own wording and adds each one here before a record uses it. Keep the list small; add tags deliberately; never change an ID once records use it.

Format: one line per tag, with an anchor so records and pages can link to it.
