# Career goals and preferences

[Career index](README.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** This page holds what you want, not what you have done: goals, motivation, how you like to work and your standard answers to portal questions. Agents use it to judge fit, to write cover-letter openings from your real motivation (never invented enthusiasm) and to fill application forms. Nothing here appears on a résumé as an achievement.

How it gets filled: your agent interviews you **one short question at a time**, using the question bank under each section. It offers the examples in brackets for you to react to, follows up on what you say ("What about it did you like?") and skips anything you want to skip. You never get the whole list at once. Each section then records when you said it and your words.

Record pattern for every section:

> Recorded {date} from your firsthand statement in {kind of session}. User-confirmed, not independently verified.
>
> {Your words, quoted.}

## Location and working arrangement

Question bank:

- Where do you live?
- Would you move for the right role? (where to, or nowhere)
- Do you prefer remote, hybrid or on-site work?
- How much travel is OK? (none, occasional, up to half the time)

{Your answers.}

## What you want next

Question bank:

- What kind of role do you want next? (a title, a type of work, a level)
- Which industries interest you?
- What size of company suits you? (startup, mid-size, large)
- Is there a problem you want to work on?

{Your answers.}

## Motivation

Cover letters draw on this, so specific reasons beat general ones.

Question bank:

- What part of your work do you enjoy most?
- Is there a cause or problem you care about personally?
- What made you choose your field in the first place?

{Your answers.}

## What has worked and what has not

The agent asks about one thing at a time, then follows up with "What about it?"

Liked:

- Which role have you enjoyed most?
- Which tasks do you enjoy? (planning, building, analyzing, teaching, selling, fixing things)
- Which ways of working suit you? (clear process, freedom to experiment, deadlines, long projects)
- Which tools or systems have you liked using?
- Which colleague or manager did you work best with?

Not liked:

- Which role did you enjoy least?
- Which tasks drain you?
- Which ways of working frustrate you?
- Which tools or systems have you disliked?
- What kind of colleague or manager is hard for you to work with?

Past jobs:

- How have promotion conversations gone for you?
- When has working with other people gone especially well?
- When has it gone badly?
- Which workspace suits you? (open plan, quiet office, home)

{Your answers.}

## Résumé presentation

How your experience is presented, as opposed to what it is. These are presentation choices, not career facts; they never change a date, title window or record.

Question bank:

- When your titles grew at one employer, should a résumé show them as one progression ("Analyst → Lead") or as separate headings, or decide per job?
- Which degrees or credentials should always appear?
- Which should appear only when they help the job? (for example a certificate from another field)
- Do you want your years of experience in a field stated in your summary? If so, from what month and year do you count, and what work counts toward it?

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Title progression | Not recorded: decide fresh for each résumé |
| Always include | Not recorded: include every relevant degree |
| Include only when relevant | Not recorded |
| Years-of-experience count | Not recorded: no figure. When recorded, store the start date and what counts, never a fixed number; work the figure out at each build |

## Documents

How your résumé and cover letters look. Defaults apply until you record something here.

Question bank:

- Use the default templates, or one of your own? (see [custom templates](../templates/custom/README.md))
- Should your résumé fit on one page, or is two fine?
- US Letter or A4 paper?
- Would you like a personal signature line at the end? (for example "Engineer • Teacher • Marathoner")

| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Résumé template | Default: templates/resume/ |
| Cover-letter template | Default: templates/cover-letter/ |
| Page length | Not recorded: one page if the evidence fits, otherwise two |
| Paper size | Not recorded: US Letter |
| Personal signature | None |

## Application answers

Standard answers for job-portal questions. They are never career achievements and never appear on a résumé or cover letter. "Not recorded" means the agent asks you each time; it never guesses.

| Portal question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Salary expectations | Not recorded: ask the user. Never fill a figure, including the posted range, unless the user gives one |
| LinkedIn | See [contact defaults](contact.json) |
| Website or portfolio | See [contact defaults](contact.json) |
| Authorized to work in {country} | Not recorded: ask the user |
| Requires visa sponsorship | Not recorded: ask the user |
| Location, working arrangement, travel | See [Location and working arrangement](#location-and-working-arrangement) |
| Start date, notice period | Not recorded: ask the user |
| How did you hear about us | Not recorded: ask the user per application |
| Voluntary self-identification (ethnicity, race, gender, veteran, disability) | Not recorded: ask the user. "Decline to answer" is a valid recorded answer |
