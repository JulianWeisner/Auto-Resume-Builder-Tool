# Career story

[Career index](README.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** This page tells your whole career in plain language, newest first. It is for people: you, interview preparation and anyone who wants the full picture quickly. It is navigation, not evidence. Every engagement links to its records, and **the records win if this page and a record ever disagree.** Numbers stay in the records, where their definitions and limits live.

How it gets filled: once your records exist, ask your agent to write this page from them. It is rewritten whenever the records change materially.
