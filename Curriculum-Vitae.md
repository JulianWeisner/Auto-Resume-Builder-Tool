# Curriculum Vitae

**Empty until you fill it.** Your working CV: a long-form summary of your whole career that you keep up to date. It starts as your imported CV; after that it changes only when you ask. Your [career records](career/README.md) take precedence over anything here that they have since corrected, and the original wording stays unchanged in the [CV snapshot](source-material/cv/README.md).
