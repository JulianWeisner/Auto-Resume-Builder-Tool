# Evidence journal

[Source index](README.md)

**Empty until you fill it.** Dated notes of longer firsthand accounts and document reviews, kept here once and linked from the records they support (a record's `evidence` field can point to an anchor below). Short corrections go straight into the record's `current_clarifications` instead.

Entry pattern:

```markdown
<a id="{topic}-{YYYY-MM-DD}"></a>
## {Topic} — {YYYY-MM-DD}

Source: {your firsthand account in which kind of session, or the document reviewed}
Verification: {user-confirmed | source-supported}
Records affected: {record IDs}

{The account or review, in your words where possible.}
```

Superseded questions stay as history; they are not open tasks.
